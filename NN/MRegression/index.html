A soil moisture dataproduct on a global scale can be derived from satellite observations of NDVI, surface temperature and backscatter by using neural network statistical tools.

ERS
Backscatter

The European Remote Sensing Satellite (ERS) scatterometer is a microwave instrument observing at a frequency of 5.25GHz. A scatterometer is an active instrument, which means it sends out a radiation pulse and measures the amount of radiation that is reflected or scattered back in the direction of the instrument. This fraction of radiation that is received at the instrument is called the backscatter and is specified in units of decibel [dB].
